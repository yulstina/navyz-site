Take Back Control: How NAVYZ’s 3rd Party Integration Helps Restaurants Own Their Customer Data

Stop losing profits and customers to third-party delivery apps. Build your own digital future.

February 24, 2025

Why third-party orders quietly cost you twice

The commission is the visible cost. The invisible one is the customer. When an order arrives through a delivery marketplace, the name, the phone number, the order history, and the reason they came back all stay with the marketplace. You cooked the food and you never learned who ate it.

Over a year that adds up to a customer list you do not own, and a marketing channel you have to rent back every month.

Bring the orders into one screen

NAVYZ third-party integration pulls marketplace orders straight into your POS and prints them like any other ticket. No second tablet chirping on the pass, no staff member retyping an order during the rush, no transposed address.

The practical effect is fewer mistakes and fewer refunds, which is usually where the real money leaks.

Then give people a reason to order direct

Once the operational noise is gone, you can start moving guests to your own ordering page. A card in the bag, a loyalty point that only counts on direct orders, or a price that is a little kinder than the app are small nudges worth repeating.

Every guest who switches is one you can reach again without paying a commission for the privilege.

What to do this month

Connect your delivery accounts to the POS so every channel prints from one place. Turn on a simple loyalty offer for direct orders. Then look at the report after four weeks and see which channel is actually growing.

You do not have to leave the marketplaces. You just have to stop letting them own the relationship.
