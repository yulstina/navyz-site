Personally Identifiable Information (“PII”) refers to information that lets us know the specifics of who you are, such as, for example:

– Your name, company, email address, phone number, billing address, and shipping address– Your BankCard Services user ID and password (if applicable)– Credit card information (if applicable)– Any account-preference information you provide us– Your computer’s domain name and IP address.

Non-Personally Identifiable Information refers to information that does not by itself identify a specific individual. Cookies

8.1. Our Site uses cookies. A cookie is a file containing an identifier (a string of letters and numbers) that is sent by a web server to a web browser and is stored by the browser. The identifier is then sent back to the server each time the browser requests a page from the server.

The cookies are typically stored on your computer’s hard drive and are used by BCS to help track your clicks as you go through the pages within a BCS website. We use cookies on our Site to recognize a computer when a user visits the Site, analyses the use of the Site, track user navigation on the web and associated mobile application (if any), administer the Site, prevent fraud and improve the security of the Site. The cookies make your use of the Site easier, make the site run more smoothly and help us to maintain a secure site.

8.2. You are always free to decline our cookies if your browser permits, but some parts of our Site may not work properly in that case.

8.3.
